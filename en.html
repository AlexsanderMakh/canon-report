Campaign activity log

Done

  • 25 Nov
    Bulgaria Search and Kazakhstan: bidding adjustments
    On 24 November the “Target impression share” strategy was tested; it reduced impressions, so on 25 November both campaigns were switched back to “Maximize clicks”. In Kazakhstan the max CPC limit was raised to €0.55 to compete in the auction; short Russian-language keywords (“printer”, “MFP”, “copier” etc.) and competitor negative keywords were added.
  • 24 Nov
    Placement and search term cleanup
    Display: excluded another 58 sites and apps (Romania — 33, Bulgaria — 12, Israel — 12, Greece — 1). Search: new negative keywords in Bulgaria (UV printers, labels, business cards, 3D) and Kazakhstan.
  • 23 Nov
    Kazakhstan: delivery started
    The delay was caused by a disconnected payment profile on the Google Ads account; once it was restored, the campaign started serving. Daily budget raised from €22 to €26 to deliver the €1,000 budget by 31 December.
  • 23 Nov
    Bulgaria Search: wider reach
    Added 24 broad keywords (copiers, document scanning, home and office printers, Black Friday offers, English-language queries); removed major electronics retailers from negative keywords so ads can show for “printer + store” searches; added protective negative keywords.
  • 23 Nov
    Romania Search: CPC towards the €0.45 target
    Average CPC since 16 November is €0.43; the max CPC limit was lowered from €0.45 to €0.40 so that the full-flight average (including launch days) reaches €0.45.
  • 23 Nov
    Display: placement cleanup, 22–23 November
    Excluded sites and apps with irrelevant traffic: Romania — 42, Israel — 21, Bulgaria — 17, Greece — 3.
  • 22 Nov
    Kazakhstan: wider reach
    Enabled Google search partners; ads now have 15 headlines instead of 10 (i-SENSYS and MAXIFY GX models added); added Russian-language keywords with matching negative keywords.
  • 22 Nov
    Display: placement cleanup, 21–22 November
    Excluded sites and apps with irrelevant traffic: Romania — 39, Bulgaria — 15, Israel — 15, Greece — 4. Search terms reviewed — no irrelevant queries found.
  • 21 Nov
    Display: placement cleanup, 19–21 November
    Excluded sites and apps with irrelevant traffic: Romania — 79, Israel — 48, Bulgaria — 36, Greece — 18. Local news and topical sites were kept. In Kazakhstan, a callout with the “%” sign that failed review was replaced.
  • 18 Nov
    Ads shown only inside campaign countries
    All campaigns now show ads only to people located in the campaign country, with no impressions outside it.
  • 18 Nov
    Search RO, BG, GR: keyword expansion
    Added broad printer and MFP keywords in local languages to reach media-plan impressions; enabled Google search partners in Romania; extended negative keywords.
  • 17 Nov
    Kazakhstan: Search campaign launched
    Per media plan: €1,000 budget, €0.35 max CPC. Ads in Kazakh and English using client-approved copy; audience — business users.
  • 17 Nov
    Display Israel and Greece: wider reach
    Added languages and small-business audiences, set max CPC limits. Israel started delivering impressions.
  • 17 Nov
    Placement and search term cleanup
    Excluded irrelevant Display sites and apps (BG, RO, GR), extended Search negative keywords. Added broader keywords to Bulgaria Search to build impressions.
  • 16 Nov
    Optimisation of all campaigns
    • Display: new small-business and office audiences, wider demographics.
    • Search: new local-language keywords, negative keywords.
    • Ad copy and callouts checked against the offer terms.
    • Daily budgets recalculated to the media plan through 31 December.
    • Max CPC limits per media plan; network and language settings.
  • 13 Nov
    Launch week
    Israel switched to a clicks strategy and unblocked; Romania Search keywords cleaned up; keyword audit in all countries.

Metric definitions
Impressions
how many times the ads were shown
Clicks
how many times people clicked the ad and went to the site
CTR
click-through rate: clicks ÷ impressions
CPC
average cost per click: spend ÷ clicks
CPM
cost per 1,000 impressions
Target
value from the Canon SMB 2026 media plan, prorated to the selected days
Display
Google Display Network: banner ads on websites and in apps
Source: Primax account (Google Ads), Canon SMB Campaign 2026 media plan. Amounts in EUR.
